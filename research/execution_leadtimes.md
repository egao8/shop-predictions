# Execution lead times: can a merchant act on a day-T odds signal?

*As of 2026-10-04. bd = business days. **[est]** = analyst estimate; **[2nd]** = secondary/marketing source.*

## Bottom line

- **Yes for POD and blank-holding DTF/DTG shops.** POD listings go live in hours; DTF shops restock in 1–3 days. Both can capture T+1…T+21 demand. POD's limit is order-to-door time (about 7–12 days), not reaction time.
- **Partly for contract screen print.** Standard is 7–10 bd; rush is 1–5 bd at +25–60% cost. Needs a 7–14-day signal or pre-orders.
- **No for licensed resellers making new product.** New Era's fastest program is 2–3 weeks. Fanatics/Nike/New Era control championship gear, which goes on sale at the buzzer.
- **Post-title demand is front-loaded** (2020 Lakers: 12 h beat the 2016 Cavs' 30 days). The actionable window is the **4–8-week playoff ramp**.

## 1. Fan-merch archetypes

**a. POD.** Publishing a design takes minutes; Printful pitches "time-to-market … 1 hour or less." Production is 2–5 bd, plus 3–4 bd standard US shipping. Printify Express does order-to-door in 2–3 bd on select SKUs. Unit cost is about $12.95 (Bella+Canvas 3001 with a front print, Printful free plan) or $7–9 (Gildan on Printify) [2nd: [podvector](https://podvector.ai/articles/printful/costs-and-charges/printful-bellacanvas-3001-price-base-cost-full-breakdown-for-pod-sellers)]. No inventory risk.

**b. In-house DTF/DTG.** SanMar ships same day from 9 DCs on UPS Ground orders placed before 4pm local. S&S has 14 warehouses with "1–2 day delivery to most of the country." Alphabroder (S&S-owned since Oct 2024, [Impressions](https://impressionsmagazine.com/news/ss-activewear-completes-acquisition-of-alphabroder/164262/)) reaches 96% of the US population next day. Ninja Transfers: "40% of our orders are Next Day shipping." Costs: Gildan 5000 blank $3.06–3.41 ([Blankstyle](https://www.blankstyle.com/gildan-5000-g500-heavy-weight-cotton-t-shirt)); DTF front print $1.50–3.60 [est]; all-in about $6–8 per shirt [est]. One heat press does roughly 60–120 shirts/hour [est].

**c. Contract screen print.** Standard is 7–10 bd after proof approval. Rush costs +25% for 4–5 bd, +50% for 2–3 bd, +60% for 1 bd. Minimums are usually 24–50 pieces per design [2nd]. With 2–5 h setup, it beats DTG only above about 24–48 units.

**d. Licensed resellers.** Nike's NBA deal runs through 2037 ([report](https://www.channelstv.com/2024/10/21/nba-nike-extend-partnership-deal-for-12-years/)). Fanatics makes fan gear itself and owns Mitchell & Ness ([Fanatics](https://www.fanaticsinc.com/global-vertical-brands)). A historical NBA license form shows a **12% royalty and a $46,000 minimum guarantee** ([Law Insider](https://www.lawinsider.com/contracts/7fcFN6oJus5)), which rules out micro-stores. The NBA files mass "Schedule A" suits against online sellers ([docket](https://www.docketalarm.com/cases/Illinois_Northern_District_Court/1--22-cv-05458/NBA_Properties_Inc._v._The_Partnerships_and_Unincorporated_Associations_Identified_on_Schedule_A/1/)), and the leagues claim team colors as protected. So small stores sell unlicensed "city/vibe" designs without logos or team names. These still carry some legal risk; "Champions" claims are the riskiest. BreakingT shows a licensed fast path: player names via players-association licenses, no team marks, 12–24 h from moment to market.

*Championship mechanics:*
- Fanatics' decorator holds both teams' graphics: "if the game ends at 11:30, we're printing at 11:31." One plant prints 34k shirts/day for overnight retail pickup.
- A limited run of both teams' locker-room gear is made in advance. Fanatics launched 300+ Knicks products at the buzzer in 2026.
- Losing-team gear goes overseas: Good360 for the NFL, historically World Vision for the NBA ([Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/29884/what-happens-losing-teams-championship-shirts)). I couldn't confirm the NBA's current partner.
- Licensed tees retail at $42–50, locker-room caps about $46 [2nd, [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/product-recommendations/style/knicks-nba-champions-winners-merch-nike-t-shirt-buy-online-1235577154/)].

**e. Ads/CRM.** Ads go live in under 24 h, but Meta needs about 50 conversions in 7 days, which means a daily budget of roughly 7× target CPA per ad set. A single budget jump over ~20% can reset learning. **What limits speed is campaign structure set up before the signal, not ad approval.** Email goes out in minutes. SMS also sends fast, but the number must be verified weeks ahead.

## 2. Local businesses

- **Food:** US Foods Pronto takes orders until 8pm for next-day delivery, 6 days a week, in 76 cities. Sysco cutoffs vary by branch (Hawaii: 4pm the day before).
- **Staffing:** Fair-workweek laws mostly cover **large chains, not independent bars**:
  - NYC: fast food (retail has separate rules); 14 days' notice; $10–$20 per change, $15–$45 inside 7 days, $75 for a cut under 24 h.
  - Chicago: restaurants with 30+ locations and 250+ employees, plus hotels; 14 days; 1 h pay per change.
  - Seattle: full-service restaurants with 40+ locations and 500+ employees.
  - Philadelphia: 250+ employees and 30+ locations ([FAQ](https://www.phila.gov/media/20231218102835/FWW-FAQ-December-2023.pdf)).
  - LA City: retail only ([ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/fair-work-week-workweek-los-angeles-city-council/12483646/)).

  Premiums are small next to playoff uplift. Near-arena spending rose 15–84% on Canadiens home playoff games ([Moneris 2026](https://dataservices.moneris.com/en/resources/blog/sports/moneris-data-for-montreal-canadiens-hockey-playoffs-2026), NHL analog). Toronto bar transactions were +76% at 11pm after Raptors ECF Game 6 [2nd, [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/1.5156192)]. Away games were flat or negative.
- **Hotels/STR:** Rates change in minutes, but often don't move. Indianapolis' 2025 Finals Game 4 rate was only **+2% vs 30 days earlier**, a "missed opportunity," while OKC hotel searches ran 45% above average ([Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4127789/nba-finals-2025-how-forward-looking-search-data-reveals-the-winning-playbook-for-hotels)).

## 3. How large Shopify/DTC stores manage inventory

- **Turns:** public apparel DTC median is 2.61/yr (about 140 days of stock): Lululemon 2.83, Levi's 2.10, FIGS 1.82. Healthy private DTC brands run 6.6–12.2 ([Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/average-cpg-inventory-turnover-public-vs-private-ecommerce-2026)) [2nd].
- **Slow decisions (6–16+ weeks), too slow for sports signals:** cut-and-sew 10–16 weeks end to end [2nd]; ocean freight 18–45 days on top of 30–60 days of production; New Era on-field 20–24 weeks. A Kansas City shop that ordered Chiefs playoff merch **8 months ahead** was stuck with thousands of units after the Jan 2026 exit ([KSHB](https://www.kshb.com/news/local-news/kansas-city-small-business-left-with-thousands-of-chiefs-postseason-merch-following-teams-early-exit)).
- **Fast decisions (hours to days):** price, ads, email, listings, domestic blank/DTF restocks, 3PL transfers. Air freight (6–10 days) costs 6–22× ocean per kg.
- **Shopify tooling:** native purchase orders and transfers, but **no native demand forecasting** ([Help](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/inventory/purchase-orders)). Stocky shut down Aug 31, 2026 ([Digit](https://www.digit-software.com/blog/shopify-stocky-being-discontinued)). Pre-orders and deposits run through the Selling Plan API and apps ([Shopify.dev](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/purchase-options/deferred)).

## 4. Early vs late

**Post-title decay (Fanatics):**
- 2026 Knicks: more than 8,000 orders/minute; top champion in any sport over the first 24 h; more than 2× the 2020 Lakers.
- 2020 Lakers: sold more in 12 h than the 2016 Cavs did in 30 days ([theScore](https://www.thescore.com/news/2038478)).
- 2024 Liberty: beat the prior WNBA 30-day record in under 24 h ([Fanatics](https://fanaticsinc.com/news/sportico-liberty-merch-becomes-fanatics-fastest-post-finals-seller)).
- During the run: 2025 Knicks sales +80% vs 2024 playoffs ([Fanatics/Bloomberg](https://www.fanaticsinc.com/news/bloomberg-new-york-knicks-merchandise-sales-skyrocket-in-nba-playoff-run)).

**Our proxy: Wikipedia daily pageviews** ([Wikimedia API](https://wikimedia.org/api/rest_v1/)). This measures attention, not sales.

| Team | Peak vs March baseline | Share of 60-day excess in first 3 d / 7 d | Playoff ramp (weekly × baseline) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 Thunder (title Jun 22) | 106× | 85% / 93% | 2–3× R2 → ~7× WCF → 22× Finals week → 1× by mid-Jul |
| 2026 Knicks (title Jun 13) | 220× | 67% / 82% | 4× R2 → 7× → 20–36× ECF/Finals → 53× → 6× next week |

**Newsvendor view.** Optimal stocking is at Cu/(Cu+Co) ([ref](https://metricgate.com/blogs/newsvendor-model-inventory-optimization/)). Estimates at a $28 tee and $46 cap:
- **POD:** Co ≈ 0, so the ratio is about 1.0.
- **DTF, pre-pressed:** cost $6.50, salvage $1–3 (5–15% of retail [2nd, [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/liquidation-items-profit-margins)]): about 0.83; about 0.9 holding blanks and transfers unpressed.
- **Rush screen print:** about 0.6–0.75.
- **Licensed cap at $23 cost:** about 0.8 for evergreen designs, about 0.55 for event-specific ones.

**Lead time, not overstock risk, is what limits fast-cycle sellers.** Overstock risk only bites on long-lead or result-specific goods.

## So what for the product

| Archetype | Act on a 1–7-day signal? | Recommend |
|---|---|---|
| POD | **Yes, same day** | Keep hidden designs per contender. On a signal: unhide, email/SMS, raise pre-warmed ad sets ≤20%/day. Push express shipping for games <10 days out. |
| DTF/DTG in-house | **Yes, 1–3 days** | Size blank/transfer reorders to the signal; pre-buy transfers for rising teams; press to order. |
| Screen-print contract | **Only at ≥7–14 days, or with rush** | Open pre-orders first, then batch-print; price rush against forecast lift. |
| Licensed reseller | **No for new product; yes for price, ads, stock moves** | Season futures for pre-playoff buys 3–24 weeks out; short signals for reallocation and markdowns. |
| Restaurant/bar | **Yes, 1–2 days** | Forecast home-game probability (Games 5–7 are "if necessary"). Order food next day. Covered chains: act ≥14 days ahead or pay small premiums. |
| Hotel/STR | **Yes, same day** | Raise rate floors and minimum stays once a home series is likely, before bookings arrive. |

**Product framing:** sell lead-time-matched playbooks, not raw odds: "action X, done by T+n, captures window Y." Route 1–7-day signals to POD, DTF, ads, hotels and bars; series-level or futures signals to screen print and licensed buys.

**Caveats:** Wikipedia measures attention, not sales; [2nd] figures are vendor or secondary claims; fair-workweek rules are simplified.
